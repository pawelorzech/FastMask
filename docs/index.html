The app

Get in, copy an address, get back to what you were doing.

Masked emails are forwarding addresses that keep your real inbox private. See who leaked or sold one, and turn it off. Your real email stays yours.

  • Every mask at a glanceGrouped by when you made it, with search and Active, Off and Archived filters.
  • One-tap copyCopy an address straight from the list.
  • Create in secondsPick a prefix, domain, note and link, or tap Random mask.
  • Switch off, archive, undoTurn masks on or off, archive them, and undo an archive. Edit the note, domain and link any time.
  • Create from anywhereUse the Quick Settings tile, the app shortcut, or share a link to FastMask.
  • Try it firstDemo mode shows everything, no account needed.

To start, create an API token in Fastmail under Settings, Privacy & Security, Integrations, with the Masked Email scope. Paste it into FastMask once and you're in. You need a paid Fastmail plan and Android 8.0 or newer.

Built so there is little to protect, then protected anyway.
