What's new in 1.10.0

• Create a mask without opening the app — share a page from the browser, use the quick settings tile, or long-press the icon. The address lands on your clipboard.
• The confirmation notification offers Open and Undo
• The sign-in screen walks you through creating the API token, and says plainly when a token lacks the Masked Email permission
• New: crash reports. If the app crashes, technical diagnostics go to Firebase Crashlytics so the bug gets fixed — never your masks, descriptions, domains, token or email
• Don't want them? Settings → Crash reports → off. Stops right away.
• Still zero tracking, no ads, no subscriptions
